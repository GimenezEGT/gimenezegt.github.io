I'm Enrico Gimenez, a molecular biologist and bioinformatician based in Belo
Horizonte, Brazil. My work sits where the wet lab meets the command line: I design
and validate molecular diagnostics, assemble and compare bacterial genomes, and
build small, reproducible tools that turn messy sequencing and lab data into
answers.

## Background

I trained as a molecular biologist and moved steadily toward computation, drawn to
the reproducibility and scale that code brings to biology. My master's research
produced the first resequenced *Corynebacterium pseudotuberculosis* genome isolated
from a camel, published as first-author work in *PeerJ* (2024). Along the way I've
contributed to peer-reviewed research in bacterial genomics, probiogenomics, and
infection immunology — from *Schistosoma mansoni* host–pathogen biology to the gut
microbiome. The full list lives in the [Publications](/#publications) section.

## What I do

- **Molecular diagnostics** — PCR/qPCR assay design, validation, and multiplexing;
  in silico primer/probe design and sensitivity/specificity checks; lab setup and
  ANVISA technical dossiers.
- **Bioinformatics & genomics** — bacterial genome assembly, comparative and
  pangenomics, variant calling for diagnostics, and immunoinformatics, working from
  public databases (NCBI/EMBL/PDB).
- **Data & tooling** — Python (pandas/numpy), reproducible pipelines, and small
  practical utilities, including AI-assisted data products. A sample of these is in
  the [Projects](/#projects) section.

## Web development

I also design and build **professional websites for personal and enterprise use** —
fast, responsive static sites deployed on free hosting, so clients run them with **no
monthly hosting cost**. Recent client work (a service business and a clinical
practice) is shown in [Projects](/#projects).

## Now

I'm currently in molecular-biology research and extending the same reproducible
mindset into IoT and electronics (MicroPython on RP2040/ESP32, FastAPI, MQTT). I
work in Portuguese (native), English, and Spanish, and I'm drawn to problems that
reward careful method and clean automation.
